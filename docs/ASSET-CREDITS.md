DM Sans and Cormorant Garamond are bundled under their SIL Open Font Licenses. Notices are in `assets/licenses/`. Blind patterns, icons and interface are original CSS/SVG/code for this concept. Colour and light previews are illustrative.

`assets/light-studio-room.jpg` is an original AI-created concept room generated on 20 October 2026 using the built-in image tool and converted to JPEG for the website. It is not an Adorn project or an installed product photograph. The UI overlays illustrative blinds and daylight treatments. The [final generation prompt](STUDIO-IMAGE-PROMPT.md) records its creation.

The four `assets/fabric-*.svg` material studies and `assets/surface-grain.svg` are original procedural SVG assets created for the concept. They use deterministic texture and lighting, and do not depict manufacturer samples.
