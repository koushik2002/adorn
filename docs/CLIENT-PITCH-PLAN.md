# Koushik's blinds website pitch plan

## Position the demo

“I build websites that help customers compare blinds, explore a look for their room and prepare a useful consultation brief.”

Introduce ADORN as an **independent, unofficial concept**. Previews are illustrative. Do not promise exact visualisation, guaranteed leads or conversion improvements.

## A two-minute walkthrough

| Time | Show | Explain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Homepage, then mobile view | “A clear visual identity gives your products a consistent presentation across phones and desktops.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Filter products and open a detail | “Customers can narrow the range before contacting your showroom.” |
| 0:45–1:15 | Change blind style, fabric, opening and daylight | “An illustrative preview makes the differences easier to discuss. Actual samples and measurements still matter.” |
| 1:15–1:35 | Complete the product finder | “A few questions give undecided customers a starting point for a conversation.” |
| 1:35–2:00 | Download a consultation brief using dummy details | “This demo creates a local brief. Your live site can send agreed enquiry details to your team once we connect and test delivery.” |

Close with: “Which products do your customers find hardest to choose, and what does your team need before a site visit?”

## Find suitable prospects

Start with showrooms, dealers and manufacturers with an approved catalogue. Look for a specific gap: products are hard to compare, phone navigation is awkward, or enquiries miss useful details. Record it before contacting anyone. Obtain permission before using their identity and assets.

Suggested outreach:

> Hi [Name], I'm Koushik. I noticed [specific observation]. I made a blinds website concept with a product explorer, room preview and consultation brief. A version for [business] could help customers understand your range before contacting you. Here's the sample: [demo link]. Would a two-minute walkthrough be useful?

## Discovery before a proposal

- Who buys: homeowners, architects, interior designers or commercial teams?
- Which products, service locations and languages matter most?
- Explain opacity, materials, operation, motorisation or installation?
- Who owns the product data and approves technical, warranty and performance claims?
- Are approved photos, fabric swatches, a logo and domain available?
- What should an enquiry include, where should it arrive and who follows up?
- Is this a consultation website or does it require pricing, ordering, payments or a dealer portal?
- What are the budget, review process and launch deadline?

## Scope options

| Package | Defined scope |
| --- | --- |
| Showcase | Responsive landing page, approved product overview/gallery, FAQs, contact links, metadata and deployment. One consolidated revision round. |
| Explore | Showcase plus a filterable catalogue, illustrative room preview, finder and one tested enquiry delivery integration. Two revision rounds and handover. |
| Connected | Separately estimated multi-page site, editable catalogue, multilingual content or a dealer/CRM integration with written acceptance criteria. |

Quote after discovery. Separate domains, hosting, subscriptions, photography, content and maintenance. State revision limits, payment milestones, support and change-request costs. These are proposed scopes, not market pricing claims.

## Delivery outline

1. Agree goals, content, scope and acceptance criteria.
2. Approve visual direction and homepage.
3. Build the catalogue, preview and consultation journey.
4. Review approved copy/assets and connect the chosen enquiry destination.
5. Test mobile, keyboard use, links, form errors and confirmed delivery; resolve issues.
6. Deploy, verify the live domain and hand over source/access instructions.

Schedule after confirming content and integrations. Publish using the [README](../README.md), record the walkthrough and track replies and proposals. Build case studies from real launches and agreed measurements.
