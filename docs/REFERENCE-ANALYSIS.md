# Reference analysis

This is an original, unofficial website concept for a blinds and shades business. It combines the product context supplied by the client with visual ideas observed in the linked website reel. It is not an official Adorn website or a pixel-for-pixel reproduction.

## What the new reel actually shows

The new reel presents a luxury car company website redesign. It is a design and lead-generation case study rather than a blinds catalogue. The following details were observed directly in decoded video frames:

- Around 14 seconds, an immersive showroom photograph sits behind the centered heading “Curated Luxury. Driven by Trust.” Two adjacent actions let visitors explore inventory or book a visit.
- A wide, inset translucent navigation bar floats above the imagery, with a menu at the left, a crest in the center, and a light contact button at the right.
- Around 17–19 seconds, dark editorial content gives way to a spacious white services section. Fine circular arcs, small numbered markers, and large areas of open space provide structure.
- Around 21 seconds, the mobile view retains the full-height showroom photograph and uses a compact floating navigation bar with a logo and hamburger menu.
- Around 25–27 seconds, another dark automotive concept uses warm architectural lighting, a left-aligned serif heading, a restrained outlined action, and a horizontal vehicle-brand strip.

The recording demonstrates scrolling and changing sections. It does not provide sufficient evidence to claim that the featured website contains an interactive 3D scene.

## Translation into the demo

The visual direction pairs midnight teal with porcelain surfaces and soft apricot accents for a distinctive, contemporary interiors showroom. Deep teal sections frame the photography, while pale backgrounds and dark text support product browsing. This palette is an original design choice for the demo; Adorn’s official brand guidelines have not been established from the references.

| Observed reference idea | Original demo application |
| --- | --- |
| Immersive showroom hero | A cinematic interior hero with a clear blinds and shades proposition |
| Floating navigation | A concise navigation layer that keeps browsing and consultation accessible |
| Paired inventory and visit actions | Explore the collection and request a consultation |
| Spacious editorial sections | Product benefits and room inspiration with generous spacing |
| Product-led presentation | Larger, filterable product cards with Room/Fabric views and a guided product finder |
| Premium showroom feeling | A refined illustrative light studio that lets visitors compare shade appearance |

The Room/Fabric controls pair reference room photography with original illustrative fabric swatches. Supplier samples and specifications must confirm actual material, colour and performance. The refined room illustration and subtle motion are original demo treatments; motion respects the system reduced-motion preference.

The light studio, product finder, and downloadable consultation brief are original demo features. They are not interactions verified in the reel. The studio is a visual illustration, not a measured simulation of daylight, privacy, or fabric performance. The brief download is local; a live launch would connect enquiry delivery to the business’s chosen channel.

## Use in a client pitch

Show the hero first, change a blind or light setting in the studio, find a suitable product, and finish by creating a consultation brief. Explain that approved branding, product specifications, real installation photography, and enquiry routing would be supplied or confirmed during a paid implementation.
